Case Briefing START HERE

TRID — the TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure rule — replaced the old GFE and HUD-1 in 2015. It governs the Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure, and generates some of the most scenario-heavy questions on the entire exam.

EXHIBIT A
What Is TRID?
Effective October 2015. Replaced the Good Faith Estimate with the Loan Estimate, and the HUD-1 with the Closing Disclosure. Governed by Regulation Z and Regulation X.
Foundation
Exam note: TRID is governed by BOTH Regulation Z (TILA) and Regulation X (RESPA) simultaneously. The LE replaced the GFE. The CD replaced the HUD-1. Know both replacements cold.

EXHIBIT B
The 6 Application Elements
Name · SSN · Income · Property Address · Estimated Value · Desired Loan Amount. When ALL 6 are present, lender must issue LE within 3 business days.
Most tested
Exam trap — the #1 TRID trap: Credit score is NOT one of the 6 elements. The exam lists it as a choice constantly. It is NOT required to trigger the LE obligation. Memorize the 6 elements without credit score.

EXHIBIT C
The 3 Tolerance Buckets
Zero tolerance — cannot increase at all. 10% aggregate — total increase capped at 10%. Unlimited tolerance — can increase freely. Which bucket a fee falls into determines whether a violation occurred.
Exam math
Exam trap: The 10% tolerance is AGGREGATE — it applies to the TOTAL of all 10% category fees combined, not to each fee individually. Also: cure window for all violations = 3 CALENDAR years from closing.

FILE 001
LE Delivery Timing
Loan Estimate must be received by the borrower within 3 business days of receiving a complete application (all 6 elements). Saturday counts. Sundays and federal holidays do not.
Critical deadline
Exam trap: The lender cannot collect ANY fees from the borrower (except a credit report fee) before the LE has been RECEIVED. Not just sent — received. If mailed, add 3 calendar days for presumed delivery.

FILE 002
CD Delivery Timing
Closing Disclosure must be received by the borrower at least 3 business days before consummation (closing). This waiting period gives the borrower time to review final terms.
Critical deadline
3 things that restart the 3-day CD waiting period: (1) APR increases by more than 0.125%; (2) Loan product changes (e.g. fixed to ARM); (3) Prepayment penalty is added. Any of these = new 3-day waiting period.

FILE 003
Zero Tolerance Fees
Cannot increase at ALL from LE to CD: Lender origination charges · Points · Transfer taxes · Required services where borrower cannot shop. Even $1 increase = violation.
Zero = zero
Exam note: Lender errors are NEVER a valid changed circumstance. If the lender estimated wrong, they absorb the cost. The borrower cannot be charged more for a zero-tolerance item just because the lender made a mistake.
▼ tap to expand exam note
FILE 004
10% Aggregate Tolerance
Recording fees and required services from the lender's approved list. The TOTAL of all 10% category fees combined cannot increase by more than 10% from LE to CD.
Math question
How to calculate: Add ALL 10% category fees on the LE. Multiply by 10%. That is the maximum allowed increase in total. If actual increase > that amount, cure = refund the excess within 3 calendar years.

FILE 005
Valid Changed Circumstances
New information about the borrower · Borrower-requested changes · Natural disasters · New flood zone designation · Interest rate lock expiration. These allow a revised LE.
Revised LE
NOT valid: Lender errors, market rate movements, information available at application but not collected. Revised LE must be delivered within 3 business days of discovering the changed circumstance.

FILE 006
LE to Closing Minimum
A minimum of 7 calendar days must pass between LE delivery and loan consummation. This can only be waived for a bona fide personal financial emergency.
7 calendar days
Exam note: This 7-day wait is separate from the 3-day CD waiting period. Both must be satisfied independently. The waiver requires documented genuine emergency — not just borrower preference or scheduling convenience.

FILE 007
Unlimited Tolerance Fees
These can increase without limit: Prepaid interest · Homeowner's insurance · Services the borrower independently shopped for. No tolerance limit applies.
No limit
Key distinction: If the borrower shops independently for a service (not from the lender's approved list), fees for that service have unlimited tolerance. The borrower takes on responsibility for cost variations when they shop independently.
▼ tap to expand exam note
FILE 008
Cure Window
Tolerance violations must be cured by refunding the excess amount to the borrower. The cure window is 3 CALENDAR years from the closing date.
3 calendar years
Exam trap: The cure window is 3 CALENDAR years — not 3 business days, not 3 months, not 3 years from discovery. From CLOSING DATE. This is a frequently tested number because candidates confuse it with other 3-day deadlines in TRID.

Three fees in the 10% category on the LE: Recording fees $200, Pest Inspection $150, Title Search $180. Total = $530. On the CD these became $220, $165, and $200. Total = $585. Is there a violation?

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • Credit score is NOT one of the 6 elements
  • LE: 3 business days from complete application
  • CD: 3 business days before closing
  • Zero tolerance: $0 increase on origination fees
  • 10% tolerance is aggregate — not per fee
  • Cure window: 3 calendar years from closing

EXAM TRAPS

  • Credit score trap — most tested TRID fact
  • Lender errors are NEVER changed circumstances
  • 10% applies to total, not each fee separately
  • 7 calendar day min between LE and closing
  • CD product change restarts 3-day wait
  • Rate movements are not changed circumstances

⚖️ Texas Section 50(a)(6) — Home Equity

80%
Max CLTV
All liens combined. Hard constitutional cap. No exceptions ever.
3%
Fee Cap
Of original principal. All fees included toward cap.
12
Calendar Days
Waiting period from written application to closing. NOT business days.
60
Days to Cure
After borrower's written notice of fee violation, lender has 60 days to refund excess.
1
Loan at a Time
Only one 50(a)(6) loan per homestead at a time.
$0
Prepay Penalty
No prepayment penalties allowed on any Texas home equity loan.
Closing location rule: Must close at lender's office, attorney's office, or title company. NEVER at the borrower's home — even if requested. Constitutional violation.
CLTV Math: Max HEL = (Home Value × 80%) − All Existing Liens. Example: $500K × 80% = $400K cap. First mortgage = $320K. Max HEL = $80,000.

🏛️ Texas Foreclosure Rules

1st Tue
Sale Day
First Tuesday of each month at county courthouse, 10AM–4PM.
21
Days Notice
Certified mail AND courthouse posting both required simultaneously.
2
Holiday Exceptions
ONLY Jan 1 and July 4 move the sale to Wednesday. No other holidays.
SCRA override: In Texas (non-judicial state), lender MUST get a court order to foreclose on active-duty servicemember's property. SCRA overrides Texas state law.

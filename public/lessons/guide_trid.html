What Is TRID?

TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure — effective October 3, 2015. TRID replaced the Good Faith Estimate (GFE) with the Loan Estimate (LE) and replaced the HUD-1 with the Closing Disclosure (CD). Governed by both Regulation Z and Regulation X.

The 6 Application Elements

#1 Most Tested TRID Fact
Complete Application Triggers LE
When ALL 6 elements are received, lender must issue Loan Estimate within 3 business days:

1. Borrower Name · 2. Social Security Number · 3. Income · 4. Property Address · 5. Estimated Property Value · 6. Desired Loan Amount

Credit score is NOT one of the 6 elements.

Key Deadlines

3
Bus. Days — LE
LE must be RECEIVED within 3 business days of complete application. Saturday counts.
7
Cal. Days Min
Minimum 7 calendar days between LE delivery and loan consummation (closing).
3
Bus. Days — CD
CD must be RECEIVED at least 3 business days before closing.
3
Cal. Years
Cure window for all tolerance violations — from closing date.

Changed Circumstances

Valid changed circumstances (allow revised LE): New borrower information · Borrower-requested changes · Natural disaster · New FEMA flood zone · Rate lock expiration

NOT valid: Lender errors in estimating fees · Market rate movements · Information available at application but not collected

CD Waiting Period — 3 Restart Triggers

  • APR increases by more than 1/8% (0.125%)
  • Loan product changes (e.g. fixed to ARM)
  • Prepayment penalty is added

Exam Traps — TRID

  • Credit score is NOT one of the 6 elements — most tested TRID fact
  • 10% tolerance is AGGREGATE — applies to the total, not each fee individually
  • Lender errors are NEVER valid changed circumstances
  • LE must be RECEIVED in 3 days — not just sent
  • Cure window = 3 CALENDAR years from CLOSING DATE
  • Cannot collect fees (except credit report) before LE is received

Key Numbers — TRID

  • 6 elements trigger LE — credit score NOT one of them
  • LE delivery: 3 business days from complete application
  • CD delivery: 3 business days BEFORE closing
  • Minimum LE to closing: 7 calendar days
  • Zero tolerance: $0 increase allowed
  • 10% aggregate: total of group cannot increase more than 10%
  • Cure window: 3 calendar years from closing date

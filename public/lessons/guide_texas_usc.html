Texas Section 50(a)(6) — Home Equity Lending

These rules are written directly into the TEXAS CONSTITUTION — making them among the most powerful consumer protections in the country. All six rules apply to every Texas home equity transaction.

The Six 50(a)(6) Rules

80%
Max CLTV
All liens combined cannot exceed 80% of fair market value. Hard constitutional cap — no exceptions.
3%
Fee Cap
Total fees cannot exceed 3% of original principal. Lender has 60 days to cure after written notice.
12
Calendar Days
Mandatory waiting period from written application to closing. Calendar days — not business days.
1
Loan at a Time
Only ONE home equity loan per Texas homestead at a time. Must pay off existing lien first.
  • Closing location: Must occur at lender's office, attorney's office, or title company — NEVER at the borrower's home
  • No prepayment penalty on any Texas home equity loan

Texas CLTV Calculation

Most Tested Texas Math
Maximum HEL Formula
Max HEL = (Home Value × 80%) − All Existing Liens

Example: $500,000 home × 80% = $400,000 cap. First mortgage = $350,000. Max HEL = $400,000 − $350,000 = $50,000

If first mortgage = $400,000 → Max HEL = $0 (no borrowable equity)

Texas Foreclosure Rules

1st Tue
Sale Day
First Tuesday of each month, 10AM–4PM at county courthouse. Jan 1 or July 4 → moves to Wednesday.
21
Days Notice
Certified mail AND courthouse posting required at least 21 days before the sale date.
Two holiday exceptions: If First Tuesday falls on January 1 or July 4 ONLY — the sale moves to Wednesday. No other holidays move the sale.

TDSML — Texas Regulator

Texas Department of Savings and Mortgage Lending regulates non-bank mortgage companies and individual MLOs. Maximum penalty: $25,000 per violation per day.

SAFE Act Mandatory Bars

  • Bar 1: Any felony within preceding 7 YEARS — any felony, regardless of type
  • Bar 2: Any felony EVER involving fraud, dishonesty, breach of trust, or money laundering — NO time limit, PERMANENT

Exam Traps — Texas USC

  • 12 CALENDAR days wait — not business days
  • 80% CLTV includes ALL liens combined
  • Closing at borrower's home = constitutional violation even if borrower requests it
  • SAFE Act Category 2 fraud felony = PERMANENT — no time limit
  • First Tuesday ONLY — not any Tuesday of the month
  • Jan 1 AND July 4 move to Wednesday — no other holidays
  • TDSML = $25,000/violation/DAY — per day structure

What Is RESPA?

The Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act — enacted in 1974, implemented by Regulation X, enforced by the CFPB. RESPA protects consumers from abusive practices in the mortgage settlement process and requires clear disclosure of settlement costs.

Section 8 — The Kickback Prohibition

Most Tested RESPA Rule
The Core Rule
No person shall give and no person shall accept any fee, kickback, or thing of value pursuant to any agreement or understanding that business incident to a real estate settlement service will be referred. NO dollar threshold — even $1 is a violation.

Things of value include: cash, gift cards, meals, entertainment, trips, software, below-market rent, discounts on personal services, and even a referral back in return for a referral.

Exam Traps — Section 8

  • There is NO minimum dollar amount — $5 gift card for a referral = violation
  • The PATTERN matters — one dinner may be OK, eight dinners each followed by a referral = kickback
  • Employer-to-employee compensation is EXEMPT — production bonuses are legal
  • Two companies trading referrals with each other = violation even with no cash

Affiliated Business Arrangements (ABA)

An ABA exists when a settlement service provider refers business to a company in which they have an ownership interest. ABAs are NOT automatically illegal but require ALL THREE conditions simultaneously:

  • Written disclosure at or before time of referral
  • Consumer is not required to use the affiliated provider
  • Only compensation is a return on the ownership interest — no per-referral payment

Servicer must RESPOND substantively within 30 business days (extendable to 45)

Maximum statutory damages per QWR violation — plus actual damages and attorney fees

Section 9 — Title Company Choice

A seller CANNOT require a buyer to use a specific title company as a condition of the sale. Penalty = 3 times the charge for the title insurance.

Section 10 — Escrow Accounts

RESPA limits the escrow cushion a lender may collect to a maximum of 2 months of projected escrow payments. Annual analysis required. Excess over 2 months must be refunded within 30 days.

Marketing Services Agreements (MSAs)

Written agreements where one settlement provider pays another for marketing services. CFPB scrutinizes heavily. Key question: does payment reflect fair market value of actual services performed? If not — disguised kickback.

Key Numbers — RESPA

  • Section 8: NO dollar threshold for violations
  • Section 9 penalty: 3× title insurance charge
  • Section 10: 2-month maximum escrow cushion
  • QWR acknowledge: 5 business days
  • QWR respond: 30 business days (45 with extension)
  • Section 8 civil damages: 3× the settlement service charge

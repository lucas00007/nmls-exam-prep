Case Briefing START HERE

Three critical consumer protection laws in one lesson. HOEPA targets predatory lending on high-cost mortgages. SCRA protects active-duty military from financial exploitation. BSA fights money laundering through the banking system.

EXHIBIT A
HOEPA — High-Cost Mortgages
Home Ownership and Equity Protection Act. Three tests: APR test, Points-and-Fees test (5%), Prepayment penalty test. ANY ONE triggers high-cost status with strict restrictions.
Predatory lending
APR thresholds: First lien: APOR + 6.5%. Second lien: APOR + 8.5%. Points/fees: 5% of loan amount (higher percentage for smaller loans). HOEPA applies to refinances on PRIMARY dwelling only — not purchases, not investment properties.

EXHIBIT B
SCRA — Military Protection
Servicemembers Civil Relief Act. Caps interest at 6% on pre-service debts during active duty. Excess interest is FORGIVEN — not deferred. Prevents non-judicial foreclosure during service + 1 year after.
Military protection
Exam trap — most tested SCRA fact: The excess interest above 6% is permanently FORGIVEN — not deferred, not accumulated, not owed later. The servicemember will never pay the waived interest. Applies retroactively to the date of the military orders.

EXHIBIT C
BSA — Anti-Money Laundering
Bank Secrecy Act. Requires SAR within 30 days of detecting suspicious activity (60 with ID extension). CTR for cash transactions over $10,000. Four AML pillars required.
AML compliance
SAR confidentiality: After filing a SAR, the institution CANNOT disclose to the subject that a SAR was filed — "tipping off" is a federal crime. Structuring = breaking up transactions to stay below $10,000 threshold — illegal even if funds are legitimate.

FILE 001
HOEPA Prohibited Terms
High-cost mortgages CANNOT have: balloon payments (most cases) · negative amortization · prepayment penalties · due-on-demand clauses · single-premium credit insurance.
Prohibited features
Exam note: If ANY of these features appears in a HOEPA-covered loan, the entire loan is illegal. Lenders must either redesign the loan to remove the prohibited features or not make the loan at all.

FILE 002
HOEPA Mandatory Counseling
Borrowers must receive HUD-approved counseling before closing a HOEPA high-cost mortgage. HOEPA disclosure must be provided at least 3 business days before closing.
Required steps
Exam note: The 3-business-day HOEPA pre-closing disclosure is SEPARATE from and in addition to the TRID CD waiting period. Both must be satisfied independently. Counseling requirement reinforces that high-cost loans require extra consumer protection.

FILE 003
SCRA Interest Cap Details
6% cap applies to ALL pre-service obligations — mortgage, car loans, credit cards. Servicemember must provide written notice and copy of orders. Cap is retroactive to orders date.
6% retroactive
Exam trap: The 6% cap is retroactive to the DATE OF THE MILITARY ORDERS — not the date the servicemember notifies the lender. The lender must refund interest above 6% back to the orders date once notified.

FILE 004
SCRA Foreclosure Protection
Non-judicial foreclosure is PROHIBITED on a servicemember's property during active duty and for 1 year after. Lender must obtain a court order to foreclose — even in non-judicial states like Texas.
Federal override
Key point: SCRA overrides STATE foreclosure procedures. Texas is a non-judicial foreclosure state — but a lender CANNOT use non-judicial process on an active-duty servicemember's property. A court order is required regardless of state law.

FILE 005
CTR — Currency Transaction Report
BSA requires a Currency Transaction Report (CTR) for cash transactions exceeding $10,000 in a single business day. Must be filed within 15 days.
$10,000 cash
Exam trap: CTR is for CASH transactions over $10,000 — not wire transfers, not checks. SAR is for SUSPICIOUS activity regardless of amount. Two different reports, two different triggers. Do not confuse them.

FILE 006
Structuring — Federal Crime
Deliberately breaking cash transactions into amounts below $10,000 to avoid CTR filing is ILLEGAL — even if the funds are from legitimate sources. This is "structuring" under 31 U.S.C. § 5324.
Federal crime
Exam trap: The legitimacy of the funds is IRRELEVANT. Structuring is illegal based on the INTENT to evade reporting requirements. A business owner depositing legitimate cash earnings in $9,500 increments to avoid CTRs is committing a federal crime.

FILE 007
SAR — Suspicious Activity Report
Filed within 30 days of detecting suspicious activity (extendable to 60 days to identify a subject). Cannot "tip off" the subject. Institution must maintain SAR confidentiality.
30-day deadline
Four BSA AML Pillars: (1) Written policies and procedures; (2) Designated compliance officer; (3) Employee training program; (4) Independent testing/audit. All four must be present for a compliant AML program.

FILE 008
GLBA Privacy
Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act requires annual privacy notices and opt-out rights for sharing non-public personal information (NPI) with non-affiliated third parties.
Privacy rules
Exam note: Opt-out applies to NON-AFFILIATED third parties only. Sharing with affiliated companies generally does not require opt-out. Exceptions include sharing for loan processing, legal compliance, and fraud prevention — these are permitted regardless of opt-out status.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • HOEPA: any ONE of 3 tests triggers high-cost status
  • SCRA: 6% cap, excess FORGIVEN not deferred
  • CTR: cash over $10,000, filed within 15 days
  • SAR: suspicious activity, filed within 30 days
  • Structuring illegal regardless of fund legitimacy
  • SCRA overrides state non-judicial foreclosure procedures

EXAM TRAPS

  • SCRA excess interest FORGIVEN — not deferred
  • HOEPA applies to refinances on primary dwelling only
  • Structuring illegal even with legitimate funds
  • SAR: cannot tip off subject — federal crime
  • HOEPA counseling + 3-day disclosure both required
  • CTR vs SAR — different triggers, different reports

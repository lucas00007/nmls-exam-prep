Know Your Exam

120
Total Questions
115 scored + 5 unscored pretest questions. You won't know which are which.
190
Minutes Allowed
3 hours 10 minutes total. About 95 seconds per question average.
75%
Passing Score
Must score 75% or higher. That means answering at least 86 of 115 scored questions correctly.
180
Day Wait
After 3 failed attempts, must wait 180 days before retesting. After each single failure: 30 days.

The Week Before — Study Plan

  • Day 7: Review all 10 interactive lessons — focus on the exam traps sections
  • Day 6: Take the full 125-question mock exam under timed conditions
  • Day 5: Review every wrong answer from the mock exam — understand WHY
  • Day 4: Focus drill on your 2 weakest topics from the mock exam
  • Day 3: Review the key numbers cheat sheet — drill until automatic
  • Day 2: Light review only — no new material. Re-read takeaways from each lesson
  • Day 1: Rest. Short review of key numbers in the morning. Early bedtime.

The Night Before

  • Confirm your exam appointment time and testing center location
  • Prepare your two valid forms of ID (see requirements below)
  • Set TWO alarms — one as backup
  • Eat a real dinner — no studying after 8 PM
  • Get at least 7-8 hours of sleep
  • Lay out your clothes and everything you need the night before

What to Bring

Required
Two Forms of Valid ID
Primary ID (one required): Government-issued photo ID — driver's license, state ID, passport, military ID

Secondary ID (one required): Credit card, debit card, or additional government ID with your name

Both IDs must have your name EXACTLY as it appears in NMLS. Name mismatches = denied entry.
Do NOT bring
Leave These at Home or in Your Car
Phone · Smartwatch · Notes or study materials · Food or drinks · Bags or backpacks · Earbuds or headphones · Calculators (one is provided on screen)

What to Expect at the Testing Center

  • Arrive 30 minutes early — late arrivals may be denied entry
  • You will be asked to empty your pockets completely
  • Palm vein scan or fingerprint required at most Prometric centers
  • You'll be given a locker for your belongings
  • A whiteboard and marker (or scratch paper) will be provided — use it!
  • You can raise your hand to leave for a break — the clock keeps running

During the Exam — Strategy

  • Read every question fully — do not skim
  • Eliminate obviously wrong answers first
  • When stuck between two answers — trust your first instinct
  • Flag questions you are unsure about and come back
  • Do not leave any question blank — wrong answers count the same as blank
  • Pace yourself: check your time after every 30 questions
  • At 60 questions remaining: make sure you have at least 60 minutes left

After the Exam

You will receive your pass/fail result immediately on screen at the testing center. A score report will also be available in your NMLS account.

If you pass: Complete your state license application in NMLS within the required timeframe. Submit employment sponsorship. You are almost there!

If you need to retake: Wait the required period, review your score report to identify weak areas, and use the full course to drill those topics specifically before retesting.

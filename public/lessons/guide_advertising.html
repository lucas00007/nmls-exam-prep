TILA Advertising — Trigger Terms

Any specific number in a mortgage advertisement that requires ALL 5 required disclosures to appear in the same ad.

What Triggers vs What Is Safe

TRIGGERS (requires all 5 disclosures)SAFE (no extra disclosures needed)
"6.75% interest rate""Competitive rates available"
"Only $1,299/month""Low monthly payments"
"5% down payment""Low down payment options"
"$300,000 loans available""Call for today's rates"
"Zero closing costs""Flexible financing options"

5 Required Disclosures When Triggered

  • Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
  • Down payment amount or percentage
  • Repayment terms
  • Amount of each payment
  • Number of payments
APR alone can be advertised without triggering all 5 disclosures. But APR must appear with equal or greater prominence than any stated interest rate.

NMLS Number Requirements

Always Required
Every Advertisement Must Include
Both the individual MLO's NMLS ID AND the company's NMLS ID must appear on ALL advertising materials including: websites · business cards · flyers · email signatures · social media posts · text messages about loan programs · any other advertising

ARM Advertising

ARM advertisements must clearly and conspicuously disclose that the rate may increase after the initial fixed period. Cannot advertise only the teaser rate without disclosing the adjustment feature.

UDAP — Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts

StandardDefinition
UnfairCauses substantial consumer injury consumers cannot reasonably avoid
DeceptiveLikely to mislead a reasonable consumer about a material fact
AbusiveTakes unreasonable advantage of consumer vulnerability or limited understanding

ANY ONE of the three is sufficient for a UDAP violation.

Common UDAP Violations

  • "Guaranteed lowest rate in Texas!" — cannot be verified
  • False urgency: "Your VA benefits expire tonight!"
  • Ads designed to look like official government correspondence
  • "No closing costs" when costs are actually rolled into rate
  • Bait-and-switch — advertising one rate, delivering a worse one

Exam Traps — Advertising

  • Social media posts are NOT exempt — same TILA rules apply
  • APR alone is safe to advertise — specific rate is NOT
  • NMLS numbers required on business cards, social posts, everything
  • APR must be equally or more prominent than any stated rate
  • False VA urgency = major CFPB enforcement priority
  • "No closing costs" must disclose how costs are actually paid

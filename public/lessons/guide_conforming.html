What Is a Conforming Loan?

A conventional mortgage that meets BOTH Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac purchase guidelines: (1) loan amount at or below the FHFA conforming limit AND (2) underwriting meets GSE standards. All conforming loans are conventional — but not all conventional loans are conforming.

2024 Conforming Loan Limits

$766,550
Standard 1-Unit
2024 standard conforming limit for 1-unit properties in non-high-cost areas
$1,149,825
High-Cost 1-Unit
2024 limit for high-cost areas (150% of baseline). FHFA sets limits annually.
Limit applies to the LOAN AMOUNT — not the purchase price. A $900K home with 20% down = $720K loan → conforming. Always calculate the loan amount.

Standard Underwriting Guidelines

  • Minimum credit score: 620
  • Maximum back-end DTI: 45% standard (up to 50% with strong AUS)
  • Maximum LTV: 97% for HomeReady/Home Possible
  • PMI required when LTV exceeds 80%

PMI Rules — Homeowners Protection Act

80%
Request Cancel
Borrower can REQUEST PMI cancellation at 80% LTV of ORIGINAL purchase price
78%
Auto-Cancel
PMI automatically cancels at 78% LTV — original purchase price, original amortization schedule
Both thresholds based on ORIGINAL purchase price and ORIGINAL amortization schedule — NOT current appraised value and NOT based on extra payments made.

Self-Employed Income

Borrowers owning 25% or more of a business = self-employed. Requires 2 years personal + business tax returns + YTD P&L. Income = 2-year average of net income from returns.

Rental Income

Only 75% of gross rent used to offset rental property PITI. 25% haircut for vacancies and expenses.

Exam Traps — Conforming Loans

  • Limit applies to LOAN AMOUNT — not purchase price
  • DU = Fannie Mae · LPA = Freddie Mac — do NOT mix up
  • PMI request at 80%, auto-cancel at 78% — two different events
  • Both PMI thresholds use ORIGINAL values — not current
  • FHA MIP does NOT cancel like PMI — must refinance to remove
  • Self-employed = 25%+ ownership — not 51%, not majority

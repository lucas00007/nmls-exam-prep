What Is TILA?

The Truth in Lending Act — enacted 1968, implemented by Regulation Z, enforced by the CFPB. TILA ensures consumers receive clear, accurate disclosures about the cost of credit BEFORE they borrow. The centerpiece disclosure is the Annual Percentage Rate (APR).

APR — Annual Percentage Rate

Most Important TILA Concept
What APR Includes vs Excludes
INCLUDED in APR: Interest rate · Origination fees · Points · MIP · Prepaid interest · Certain broker fees

NOT included: Appraisal · Title insurance · Recording fees · Transfer taxes · Homeowner's insurance

APR always equals or exceeds the note rate.

Trigger Terms

Any specific number in a mortgage advertisement that requires ALL 5 disclosures to appear in the same ad:

  • Specific interest rate
  • Specific monthly payment amount
  • Specific loan amount
  • Specific down payment amount or percentage
  • Specific closing cost amounts
5 Required Disclosures when triggered: APR · Down payment amount or % · Repayment terms · Amount of each payment · Number of payments

Exam Traps — Trigger Terms

  • APR ALONE can be advertised without triggering all 5 disclosures
  • "As low as 5%" IS a specific number — it triggers
  • "Competitive rates" is vague — it does NOT trigger
  • APR must appear with equal or greater prominence than any stated rate
  • Social media posts are NOT exempt — same rules apply

Right of Rescission

3
Business Days
Standard rescission period after closing. Saturday counts; Sundays and federal holidays do not.
3
Years
Extended rescission if proper notice was NOT given at closing. Runs from closing date.

Applies to: Refinances on primary residence · HELOCs on primary residence · Home equity loans on primary residence

Does NOT apply to: Purchase money mortgages · Vacation homes · Investment properties · Commercial property

Ability to Repay (ATR) Rule

Lenders must make a reasonable good-faith determination that the borrower can repay before originating. Must verify at least 8 factors: income, assets, employment, mortgage payment, other mortgage obligations, other debts, DTI, credit history, housing costs.

Credit score alone is NEVER sufficient to satisfy ATR. Actual income verification is required.

Qualified Mortgage (QM)

Key Numbers — TILA

  • Rescission: 3 business days (primary residence refi only)
  • Rescission extended: 3 YEARS if notice not given at closing
  • QM DTI maximum: 43%
  • QM fee cap: 3% of loan amount (loans ≥ $100K)
  • QM maximum term: 30 years (40-year = NOT QM)
  • Business days for rescission: all days except Sundays and federal holidays

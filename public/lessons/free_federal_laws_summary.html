RESPA — Quick Reference

Section 8 — Most Tested
No Kickbacks Rule
Never give or receive anything of value in exchange for a referral of settlement service business. NO dollar threshold — even $5 is a violation. The EXCHANGE is the violation, not the amount.
Key Numbers
RESPA Numbers
Section 9 penalty: 3× title charge · Max escrow cushion: 2 months · QWR acknowledge: 5 business days · QWR respond: 30 business days

TILA — Quick Reference

Trigger Terms
What Triggers Full Disclosure
Specific rate · Specific payment · Specific loan amount · Specific down payment · Specific closing costs. Any ONE triggers all 5 required disclosures: APR, down payment, repayment terms, payment amount, number of payments.
Key Numbers
TILA Numbers
Rescission: 3 business days (primary residence refi only) · Rescission if no notice: 3 YEARS · QM DTI: 43% max · QM fees: 3% of loan amount · QM term: 30 years max

TRID — Quick Reference

The 6 Application Elements: Name · SSN · Income · Property Address · Estimated Value · Loan Amount. Credit score is NOT one of them — this is the #1 TRID exam trap.
Key Deadlines
TRID Timeline
LE delivery: 3 business days from complete application · CD delivery: 3 business days BEFORE closing · Min LE to closing: 7 calendar days · Cure window: 3 CALENDAR years

HOEPA — High-Cost Mortgage Triggers

ANY ONE of three tests triggers HOEPA high-cost status:

  • APR test: First lien: APOR + 6.5% · Second lien: APOR + 8.5%
  • Points-and-fees test: Fees exceed 5% of loan amount
  • Prepayment penalty test: Penalty exceeds certain thresholds
Prohibited in HOEPA loans: Balloon payments · Negative amortization · Prepayment penalties · Due-on-demand clauses · Single-premium credit insurance

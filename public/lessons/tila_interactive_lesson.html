Case Briefing START HERE

TILA — the Truth in Lending Act — has protected consumers from hidden loan costs since 1968. Your job: know it cold before you walk into the NMLS exam room.

EXHIBIT A
What Is TILA?
Enacted 1968. Implemented by Regulation Z. Enforced by the CFPB. Mission: ensure borrowers receive clear, accurate cost disclosures before they borrow.
Foundation
Exam note: TILA transferred from the Federal Reserve to the CFPB in 2011 under Dodd-Frank. If the exam asks who enforces TILA today — the answer is always the CFPB. Not the Fed. Not HUD. The CFPB.

EXHIBIT B
APR — The Core Disclosure
Annual Percentage Rate = note rate + fees. Always ≥ the note rate. Gives borrowers a standardized way to compare the true cost of different loan offers.
Exam critical
Included in APR: Note rate, origination fees, points, PMI/MIP, prepaid interest, broker fees. NOT included: Appraisal, title insurance, recording fees, transfer taxes, homeowner's insurance. APR is always ≥ note rate because of the added fees.

EXHIBIT C
Right of Rescission
3 business days to cancel after closing — primary residence refinances only. NOT purchase loans. Not vacation homes. Not investment properties.
Most tested
Exam trap: If the lender fails to give proper Notice of Right to Cancel at closing — rescission extends from 3 days to 3 YEARS. This is the nuclear consumer protection option and is frequently tested.

Evidence Files STUDY

FILE 001
Trigger Terms
Any specific rate, payment, loan amount, or down payment % in an ad triggers ALL 5 required disclosures in that same ad.
Advertising rule
Exam trap: APR alone can be stated without triggering all disclosures. But a specific interest rate requires APR to appear with equal or greater prominence. "Low rates available" = safe. "6.5% rate" = triggers everything.

FILE 002
The 5 Required Disclosures
When triggered: (1) APR · (2) Down payment amount or % · (3) Repayment terms · (4) Amount of each payment · (5) Number of payments.
All 5 required
Exam trap: All 5 must appear in the SAME advertisement. Not on a separate page. Not hyperlinked. Not in a tiny footnote. "Clear and conspicuous" means comparable size and placement to the trigger term itself.

FILE 003
Business Day Counting
For rescission: business days = all days EXCEPT Sundays and federal holidays. Saturday COUNTS. Closing day = Day 0 (doesn't count).
Heavily tested
Exam trap: Saturday counts! Most candidates assume it doesn't — it does. If a federal holiday falls in your 3-day count, skip it and push the deadline one more day. Only federal holidays count — NOT state holidays.

FILE 004
ATR — Ability to Repay
Lenders must verify 8 factors before originating any mortgage: income, employment, assets, mortgage payment, other mortgages, monthly debts, DTI, and credit history.
Dodd-Frank
Exam trap: Credit score alone is NEVER sufficient for ATR. You must actually verify income and employment. Approving based only on a 760 credit score without verifying income = clear ATR violation, regardless of the score.

FILE 005
QM — Qualified Mortgage
Safe harbor for ATR claims. Standard QM requires: max 43% DTI, 3% points/fees cap, 30-year max term, no balloon payments, no negative amortization.
Safe harbor
Exam trap: 40-year loans are NOT QM — the maximum term is 30 years. Standard QM = conclusive safe harbor. Higher-priced QM = rebuttable presumption only (weaker protection). Know the difference.

FILE 006
APR Advertising Rules
APR alone can be safely stated. If a specific rate is stated, APR must be equally prominent. Tiny footnote APR next to headline rate = violation.
Clear & conspicuous
ARM rule: For adjustable-rate mortgages, the APR must disclose that the rate may increase after the initial fixed period. You cannot advertise only the low teaser rate without disclosing that it adjusts.

FILE 007
Rescission Exceptions
No rescission right for: purchase money mortgages, vacation homes, investment properties, commercial properties. Primary residence refinances ONLY.
Exam trap
Exam trap: Borrower refinances their vacation home and calls 2 days later to rescind — no TILA right. The exam adds details like "used 4 months per year" or "considers it a second home" — doesn't matter. Principal dwelling = primary residence only.

FILE 008
3-Year Extension
If the lender fails to provide proper Notice of Right to Cancel at closing — rescission extends from 3 business days to 3 YEARS.
Nuclear option
Exam note: Only the BORROWER can waive the rescission right — and only in a genuine documented emergency. If the exam shows a borrower trying to rescind 18 months after closing, check whether proper notice was given. If not — rescission may still be valid.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • APR always ≥ note rate (includes fees)
  • Trigger terms require all 5 disclosures
  • Saturday counts for rescission counting
  • Rescission = primary residence refi only
  • ATR: credit score alone is never enough
  • QM max: 43% DTI · 3% fees · 30 years

EXAM TRAPS

  • Purchase loans — NO rescission right
  • 40-year loans — NOT QM
  • APR alone is safe to advertise
  • Miss proper notice → 3-year extension
  • Vacation homes — no rescission right
  • Higher-priced QM = rebuttable only

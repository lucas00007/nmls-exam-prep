Core MLO Professional Duty

MLOs owe a duty of good faith and fair dealing to borrowers. This means: no misrepresentation, no steering to benefit the MLO at the borrower's expense, and honest disclosure of all material facts. This duty applies even when honoring it costs the MLO money.

The ethical rule that answers most exam questions: The borrower's best interest comes first — before commission, before company quota, before personal convenience.

LO Compensation Rule

Regulation Z — Not RESPA
Anti-Steering Prohibition
MLOs cannot steer borrowers to loan products that do not serve their interests in order to earn higher compensation. When Product A better serves the borrower and Product B pays the MLO more — the MLO must recommend Product A.

Dual compensation prohibited: Cannot receive compensation from BOTH the lender AND the borrower on the same transaction.

UDAP Standards

StandardDefinitionIntent Required?
UnfairCauses substantial injury consumers cannot reasonably avoid, not outweighed by benefitsNo
DeceptiveLikely to mislead a reasonable consumer about a material factNo
AbusiveTakes unreasonable advantage of consumer vulnerability or relianceNo

ANY ONE of the three is sufficient for a violation. All three are enforced by the CFPB.

Professional Ethics Rules

  • Occupancy fraud: Knowingly processing a loan with false occupancy = federal mortgage fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1014). Friendship with borrower provides zero protection.
  • Document fraud: Suspected falsified documents = STOP and REPORT to compliance immediately. Proceeding with documentation of concerns is NOT protection.
  • Net tangible benefit: Every refinance recommendation must benefit the borrower. Break-even = closing costs ÷ monthly savings. If break-even exceeds hold period → refi does not serve borrower.
  • ABA disclosure: Financial interest in a referred service must be disclosed in writing. Non-disclosure = RESPA AND ethics violation simultaneously.
  • Expired license: Must stop originating IMMEDIATELY when license expires. No grace period. Each transaction while unlicensed is a separate violation.

Fair Lending Service Quality

Fair lending applies to ALL aspects of the origination process — not just final credit decisions. Providing materially different service quality (response times, thoroughness, follow-up) to borrowers based on protected class = fair lending violation even when all loans ultimately close.

Elder Financial Exploitation

Warning signs: unusual urgency · online-only investment contacts · large equity at risk · borrower confusion. MLOs have ethical (and in many states legal) obligations to pause before proceeding with transactions showing exploitation warning signs.

Exam Traps — Ethics

  • LO Compensation Rule = Regulation Z — NOT RESPA Section 8
  • Cannot receive compensation from both lender AND borrower
  • Documenting fraud concern while proceeding = participation in fraud
  • UDAP abusive standard targets exploitation of vulnerability
  • Service quality disparities = fair lending violation even if all loans close
  • Reporting discrimination is the RIGHT action — not a violation
  • Ethics = 16% of the NMLS exam — do not underestimate it

Ethics Quick Rules

  • Borrower interest first — always
  • Suspected fraud: STOP and REPORT — never proceed
  • Expired license: stop IMMEDIATELY — no grace period
  • LO Comp: cannot steer for higher pay
  • Dual comp: lender OR borrower — never both
  • UDAP: unfair + deceptive + abusive — any one is enough
